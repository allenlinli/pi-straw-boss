# pi-straw-boss 0.60.2

Not yet released.

## Zen: the head tower's strip starts at rest

Boss ruling 2026-11-06, on seeing three of the head tower's last lines under the breathing circle the moment Zen opened: the head tower is the main tower, so Zen should default to the breath, and the conversation should show when the input is clicked.

- **Every visit starts at the breath.** On a page load and on every visit, Zen shows the breathing circle, its line on mindfulness, and the folded input, with none of the head's lines (they were the last three, and "on it…" while it worked).
- **You call the lines up.** A click or tap on the input, a focus on it, **Enter**, or **H** brings the head's last lines up, clear for 20 seconds from then (the first H; the second opens the whole conversation, and the first H with no lines opens it at once). **Esc**, a tap on the blank space, or the focus leaving the input (an empty input that loses it) rests the strip again. Closing the whole conversation (Esc, H, *Back to the breath*, a gesture down) returns to the rest, not to the lines.
- **What still comes by itself.** A reply to a message you sent in this visit. A wait or question for you does not: it is a card, counted in the line above the input (below), and Enter calls it up. The head's own lines (a tower's answer, a report it starts itself) never wake the strip, and what the page already held when the visit began does not either.
- **Unchanged.** Standard and Geek; the fade of a line; the history; the palette entries; the keys.
- **Demo.** `?zen=head` shows the rest, `?zen=head-open` the lines and the input, `?zen=head-history` presses H twice; `?zen=head-rest` no longer presses Esc.

Design: `docs/specs/2026-11-04-head-tower.md`, **Rest is the default**.

## Zen: cards wait for Enter

Boss decision 2026-11-06 (Z-D19), the same principle one step on: 「如果 user 還在呼吸，下一張卡片也可以先 hold on，除非 user enter 願意去接卡片」, and for every kind of card, 「全部都等 Enter」.

- **No card takes the screen by itself while Zen rests on the breathing circle:** a unit's dialog, a trust question, a question a box forwarded, a teammate's confirm, a tower's wait, a pair request, a proposal. They queue, blocking questions first, and make no sound and no motion.
- **One quiet line above the input** says how many wait: 「1 張卡等 Enter。不急，等你準備好。」 / "1 card waits for Enter. No rush, when you're ready." (Zen's one count; the head tower's own waits are counted in it and no longer wake the strip). **Enter**, or a tap on the line, takes the next card. When you answer or put it aside, Zen breathes again and the next card waits for Enter. While a card waits, the input's hint says to click, since Enter takes the card first.
- **A card on the screen is never interrupted.** Z-D18's preemption (a blocking question taking the screen from an untouched wait, the 20 s touched window, the parked card, the 500 ms look, and `gui/zentouch.ts`) is gone. With a session for company a card still takes the screen by itself.
- **A page nobody is looking at** (hidden, or in front without the focus) gets one system notification for each card, once for as long as the server lists it: a card that was on the screen, put aside, or left behind a hidden page does not get a second one.
- **Nothing moves when a card comes or goes.** The input's strip keeps two rows under the lines, the link's and the cue's, in every state; the circle gives way (down to 80 px) when the circle, its line, and the strip are taller than the page, and starts under the *Working · Idle* line at the top, so a three-line maxim no longer puts the circle behind it at 1280 × 800, 1280 × 720, or on a phone. The cue's words are written only when they change, so a screen reader is not told again.
- **An ally base's unit-status report and a Link notice are not cards in Zen** (they never were; a test now pins it, and the demo's `?ally=report` shows it). A question a box forwards is one, and waits for Enter.
- **Demo.** The card states press Enter two seconds in so they open on their card; `&queued` leaves the cards waiting.

Design: `docs/specs/2026-11-01-zen-view.md`, **Cards wait for Enter**.
