# pi-straw-boss 0.61.1

Not yet released.

## The comms log no longer pulls a reader back to the end (todo #123)

With the comms log following new output, a small scroll up was undone by the next entry, and a wheel or trackpad fling that started at the end could be pulled back in the middle of its own motion: the log counted a reader as "at the end" until they were more than 80 px away (the end sentinel's margin), and only a finger on the log (touch) held it. A flick of the wheel, the first pixels of any scroll up, and the arrow keys all raced the next entry.

- A user's move up (a scroll event the log did not make itself) leaves the end at once: the log stops following, the "N new" button appears, and entries that arrive do not move the reader. Scrolling back to the very end follows again, as does crossing back into the sentinel's margin.
- A wheel up, an Up, Page Up, or Home key, a finger, and the fling after them hold the log until 250 ms pass with no input and no scroll; one that never left the end then lands there in one jump. A wheel down at the end holds nothing: an entry lands at once, so the tail of the fling has no new room to glide into.
- A log that shrinks under its end (an entry takes its real height) clamps the browser's scroll offset to the end, which is not a move up; the resize observer resyncs the log's own record of its offset even while a finger holds it.

Traces from a real Chrome (a wheel scroll up of 50 px with entries arriving every 300 ms: the log was back at the end a second later before, and stays 50 px above it after; the up, down, and tail flings and the touch flings are unchanged on a desktop width, a phone width, and Geek) are kept with the review notes of this change. Headless Chrome cannot make a trackpad's momentum phase, so the wheel traces use decaying deltas as the stand-in; `tests/chat-scroll.test.ts` drives the real mount of the log with the same sequences.
