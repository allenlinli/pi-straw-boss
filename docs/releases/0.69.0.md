# pi-straw-boss 0.69.0

Not yet released.

## Raven

- **The lightning and thunder come to a watcher** (todo #138). The first strike came 57.6 s after the backdrop showed, but the page calms after 30 s without input, so a boss who watched without touching never saw a flash or heard thunder. Now the first strike comes 7 s after the backdrop shows, then every 61 s, and the perched raven's head turn first comes about 4 s after it shows (it was 11 s). On a fine pointer (a mouse or trackpad) calm no longer stops the strike, the raven's light and its head turn, or the thunder; the rain still rests under calm. A phone (coarse pointer) keeps the old rule: calm stops all of it, for the battery. Hidden tabs, Sound off, and reduced motion are as before. The cost: on the map the strike is a layer's opacity (the compositor, no main-thread work); on the head page it is an animated number, so the page ticks one animation per frame while calm.
- **A phone shows the castle** (todo #138). On a phone the 1280 px picture was a 220 px strip under the header, covered by the cards. Under 600 px the picture now covers the screen's height and is pinned left, where the moon and the castle are; the gate on the right falls off the edge. The map's perched raven shows on a phone too (44 px, on the head band, taking no tap); the head page's stays off it, since it sits on the gate.
- **Frosted glass** (todo #138). Where the browser supports `backdrop-filter`, Raven's large panels (a base on the map, the comms and the head page, the dossier and timeline, a settings group, a sheet) take a translucent fill and blur the picture behind them (8 px), so the castle shows through; without it they keep the opaque fills. Text keeps 4.5 : 1 over the brightest part of the picture at the lightning's peak (measured). Standard and Geek do not change.
