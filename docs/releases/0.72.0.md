Not yet released.

## Wait cards in the terminal

Todo #144 (spec `docs/specs/2026-11-10-wait-cards-tui.md`), tasks 1 to 4: the cards that wait for you are shown and pressed in a terminal, with typed keys only. The GUI's question panel (task 5) follows `feat/auto-mode-gui`.

- **`/boss wait [-w workspace | label | head]`** lists every open card of every tower and the head (this tower's first) and presses one from select dialogs, with no `W<n>` typed: done, an option, skip (a reason, then a confirm), reply to the tower, or start entering a secret. It sends the page's own `press` op.
- **The stand-in prompt** prints a block of its own tower's open cards under the state line when they change, and `/wait` (or `/boss wait`) takes the keyboard to press one: this prompt has no dialogs, so a number picks the card, a number its act, and a skip's confirm is `y/N`. A paste, a run that starts, and a pane that cannot be marked press nothing and post nothing.
- **A live run's pane** takes `/boss wait` typed by the boss, with a widget of the open cards. The run's token is never sent, and a run's model can press nothing.
- **Same guards as the page.** The picker runs under the here mark for the whole mode; the secret's first step is offered only at this computer's keyboard and not under auto mode; `boss-waits` and `tower-memory` stay refused to every run token; card text is cleaned again at every reader.
- **The press log records the door** (`via=pi`, `prompt`, `run-pane`, `gui`), never the text.

## Wait cards in the page

Task 5 of todo #144 (spec `docs/specs/2026-11-10-wait-cards-tui.md`, section 4): a tower's wait for the boss is drawn as the question panel the alert confirm card is, in Standard, Geek, Raven and Zen.

- **One card look.** The card is built of the confirm card's parts (`gui/askview.ts`, shared with `gui/ask.ts`): a head (the kind's tag, who raised it, the *Parked for your return* mark of auto mode), what to do as the question, the acts as a radiogroup, **Send**, and a status line. The `.wait-card .wc-*` look rules are gone; every view's `.ask` rules apply, and Raven leaves the double rule and ticks off a card nested in the tower card.
- **Every press is pick, then Send.** A tap on a row only marks it (nothing is preselected); Send presses. **Skip** is a row: picking it shows who is told and an optional reason, and Send declines. **Reply to the tower** is a row: picking it shows the box, and Send (or Enter) tells the tower. A row folded into the tower card, its comms or the head page has no inline button; a tap unfolds the panel. The return panel's cards press the same way.
- **Phone.** Every row, Send and link of a card is 44 px tall with 8 px between, in all four views.
- **The head tower's card says so.** 主塔台 in Traditional Chinese and 鴉閣 in Raven (`_head` twins of 15 messages), where a workspace's card says 塔台 and 管家. Raven English keeps the base words.
- **Demo.** `?waits=cards&head=waiting` adds the head tower's own card.
