# pi-straw-boss 0.60.1

Not yet released.

## The wait panel's buttons

On a phone, the head tower's page showed a wait with buttons that seemed to do nothing.

- **No link to the page you are on.** "Open the tower's comms" on the head tower's page, and on a workspace tower's own comms, led to that same page, so it is no longer drawn there. The tower's card on the overview and the workspace page keeps it.
- **The link is a button.** `a.pair-btn` sat 7 px high and underlined next to its sibling buttons; it is now centred in the same 44 px box with no underline.
- **Dismiss and Send say what happened.** A dismissal the server took folds the panel and says "This wait was dismissed." for a few seconds under the rows (the row itself goes with the next snapshot); an answer says "Sent to the tower." and keeps Handle; a refusal still shows its reason. While the stream is stale the panel says the page is offline and why Dismiss and Send are off. English and Traditional Chinese.

## The head tower's page is the conversation

Boss decisions 2026-11-06 (A / A / A). The head page carried a workspace tower's whole comms page; most of it meant nothing for a head that does no work.

- **One page, one job.** The page is the head's conversation and its composer. **End conversation**, **New conversation**, and **Close** sit in one row above the composer; the `⋯` popover, the conversation line, and the `事件執行・N` bar are gone from it. The memory footer shows only when the head holds a standing instruction, a decision, or a pending proposal (not for `0・0・0`, not for waits alone). The head's tool calls (`tower_done`, `tower_send`), shell lines, and the sb server's notices and timers are no longer drawn; its words, yours, and the towers' answers stay.
- **Asks are one row per broadcast.** `I'm up → 4 towers · 2h ago · 1/4 replied`; a tap unfolds each tower's newest answer as text, with a link to that tower's comms, or a muted "no tower" for a workspace without one. A tower reads waiting, answered, or no answer (24 h): "answered" appears only with an answer (it used to mean "older than a day"). Groups quiet for 24 h fold into "Older (n)". `asks.json` gains `group` (the id of the first ask of the broadcast); an older file still reads and is grouped the same way (same first line, within 60 s).
- **A wait stops showing when it lapses.** A boss wait is shown until its own `due` (with none, for a day); the head's snapshot summary leaves lapsed ones out, so the page, the Zen strip, and the alerts agree. They stay in `memory.json` for 7 days and `/boss memory -w head` lists them. The head's brief names them ("Lapsed waits … resolve each, or ask the boss again with a new due"), so it closes or renews them when it wakes. A workspace tower's waits are as they were.
- **Demo.** `?head=answers` and `?head=waiting` carry five broadcasts of four workspaces (one with no tower), a lapsed and a fresh wait, event runs, and the items the page hides.

Design: `docs/specs/2026-11-06-head-page-minimal.md`.

## Fixes

- **A session's divider no longer lands on the strips above the composer.** In the comms (Geek and Standard, phone and desktop) the hairline above the composer sat on the bottom edge of the last todo or monitor strip, so the strip's border and the divider collided and the strip looked clipped. The divider now sits under the log, above the docked strips (queue, todos, monitors), with a 6 px gap under it and above the composer. A composer with no strip above it keeps its divider where it was. Tap targets are unchanged.
- **A queued message's label stays in one piece.** On a phone the label of a queued row (排隊中・插話, 排隊中・做完再送, Queued · steer, Queued · follow-up) broke in the middle (插 | 話) when the message beside it was a long path or URL. The label no longer wraps; the message breaks where it must.
