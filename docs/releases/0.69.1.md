## Raven

- **The perched raven turns its head without a second head beside it.** The profile stayed under the turned frame, so its beak showed beside the turned head, and the 0.7 s dissolves drew both birds at once. The profile now goes out as the turned frame comes in, in 0.17 s, and the turned head holds for about 4 s.
- **The flying raven crosses at one speed.** Its path crept across the screen for ten seconds and then shot off it in under one, seven times as fast. It now crosses in 12.5 s at one speed and glides gently up and down as it flies.
