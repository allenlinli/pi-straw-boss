# pi-straw-boss 0.69.0

Not yet released.

A main agent now has a kind, and the dispatch rules come with the plan (todo #137, phase 1; spec `docs/specs/2026-11-09-agent-kinds.md`).

## Kinds

- **`boss_duty`** sets a main's kind: `coordinator` (the default), `integrator`, or `standing`, with a one-line reason. The kind is the prompt the main runs, it shows on the card, comms and dossier (協調者 / 整合員 / 值守 in every theme, with the plan's graph after it), and a switch is an event on the timeline and a line in the tower's digest.
- **Integrators keep merging.** A main named `integ <project>` (at start or at any rename) or holding `train` todos is migrated to `integrator` once, with an entry saying why. Today's ledger integrators (`integ FE2`, `integ v3`) and takeovers that rename themselves get the integrator prompt without doing anything. Any other integrator calls `boss_duty integrator`. A declared kind is never migrated; with integration off nothing is migrated and an integrator runs as a coordinator.
- **A `train` todo needs the integrator kind.** Ledger merges and deploys are never gated. Switching away from `integrator` is refused while `train` todos are pending or in progress; ledger trains are not seen, so a ledger integrator can switch away mid-train, and then the boss or the tower hands its trains on.
- **The sidebar** sorts a member beside the main of its tab that publishes `sb_duty=integrator` (display only; a forged token misplaces one row). The yard card follows the kind: a writer of another kind shows "changed kind".
- **Wording:** the slip's `coordinator` field keeps its name; the prose and the yard message now say "the slip's sender" (送車者).

## Dispatch rules follow the plan

- **A coordinator main that dispatches a `worker` (or an unknown role, or a spawn with no role) must first write a plan whose todos name a graph** (`metadata.graph`: `single-loop`, `fan-out/fan-in`, `orchestrator-worker`). Scouts, planners, reviewers, and `subagent_resume` need none, and integrators and standing mains are never asked. The first refused call says how: write the plan's todos, or, for a main whose job is a project's merge trains or minding one system (a v1 redis watch, a CR9 switch), call `boss_duty` with `integrator` or `standing` instead. A main named `integ <project>` is migrated before the gate runs.
- `main.md` is shorter; the dispatch rules moved to `dispatch.md` and two graph addenda that sb adds to a coordinator's prompt once its todos name a graph (one steer when it changes). An integrator and a standing main always carry `dispatch.md`.
- `planning.md` asks a single loop that a worker carries to write `"graph": "single-loop"`.

## Operate and link

Operate controllers see the kind word and the graph in the snapshot, and the `duty` event. The ally board carries neither.

## Not in this release

## Sound

- **Background sound and sound effects are two settings** (todo #139; spec `docs/specs/2026-11-09-sound-split.md`). The settings page's one Sound switch and Volume are now **Sound effects** (音效) and **Background sound** (背景音), each with its own switch and volume. Sound effects is the cues: the question, finish, and failure clips (a theme's own included), the unit-ready chime, Zen's bowl and knock; it keeps its Try button. Background sound is what plays under the page: Zen's music, and Raven's rain and thunder (the thunder belongs to the weather). Each switch silences only its class and each volume scales only its class. Both are off by default, work in Standard, Geek, Zen, and Raven Keep, and Zen's corner menu and palette carry both.
- **Your old setting carries over.** A browser that had Sound on starts with both on at the old volume; one that had it off starts with both off. The old keys are only read, never written.
- **Zen's music needs Background on.** The ♪ still picks whether Zen plays music, but with Background off it plays nothing, fetches nothing, and says so (*Music: on, but background sound is off*); a playing track stops when Background is turned off and starts again when it is turned back on. A device that had Sound off and the ♪ on hears no music until Background is turned on.
- Background has no Try button: what it plays is already audible while you move its volume.
