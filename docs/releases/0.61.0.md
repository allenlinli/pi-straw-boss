# pi-straw-boss 0.61.0

## An admin stops one main on a shared box (the controller's side)

A controller that is an admin of a team can ask an unattended box to stop one of its mains ([spec](../specs/2026-11-07-link-remote-stop.md)). The box's side (the `unit-stop` kind, the authority check, the `mains` of `unit-status`, the `interrupt` mode) came first; this is what the boss sees and types. The box decides and tells the result in a notice.

- **`/boss link units [box]`** lists the units each box reported and, under each, the mains this computer may stop: name, state, who dispatched it (when it was not this computer), and the first six characters of its handle. A box that takes no stop says so.
- **`/boss link stop <box> <main | handle start> [--interrupt] [reason]`** resolves the main by name (any case) or the start of its handle, asks a confirm on this computer only (`hereOnly`) that names the box (label and short id), the main, its unit, its dispatcher and the mode, and posts `link/unit-stop`. `--interrupt` counts anywhere in the line as a whole word. A reason over 200 bytes is refused before the confirm. A box in two teams is read from its freshest status row, as the page does. Both commands have Tab completion.
- **The Mains fold on an ally card.** Each unit's fold lists its mains with a **Stop** button (44 px each way on a phone). The sheet has Cancel (first, focused), **Stop** (quit when idle) and **Interrupt and stop**, which is drawn heavier (a solid fill and a 2 px border) because it aborts the turn in progress. The row then reads *stopping…* until the box says so. A paired phone may stop (`link/unit-stop` is in `REMOTE_LINK`). A box without `unit-stop` in its caps, a stale status, or a main already stopping shows no button.
- **What a teammate wrote is shown safely.** Labels, names, units, states and handles in `link units`, the candidate lists, the stop confirm and the sent note go through the same escaping as the other `/boss link` screens (`src/safetext.ts`: control, bidi and invisible characters as `\x1b`, `\u202e`; a line break as `\n`), so a box cannot repaint the confirm the boss approves or write a terminal sequence such as OSC 52.

Wire: `link/unit-stop {team, device, handle, mode, reason}` is a new control route (a paired device may post it). Nothing else changes for this side.
