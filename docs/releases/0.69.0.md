# pi-straw-boss 0.69.0

Not yet released.

## Raven

- **The lightning and thunder come to a watcher** (todo #138). The first strike came 57.6 s after the backdrop showed, but the page calms after 30 s without input, so a boss who watched without touching never saw a flash or heard thunder. Now the first strike comes 7 s after the backdrop shows, then every 61 s, and the perched raven's head turn first comes about 4 s after it shows (it was 11 s). On a fine pointer (a mouse or trackpad) calm no longer stops the strike, the raven's light and its head turn, or the thunder; the rain still rests under calm. A phone (coarse pointer) keeps the old rule: calm stops all of it, for the battery. Hidden tabs, Sound off, and reduced motion are as before. The cost: on the map the strike is a layer's opacity (the compositor, no main-thread work); on the head page it is an animated number, so the page ticks one animation per frame while calm.
- **A phone shows the castle** (todo #138). On a phone the 1280 px picture was a 220 px strip under the header, covered by the cards. Under 600 px the picture now covers the screen's height and is pinned left, where the moon and the castle are; the gate on the right falls off the edge. The map's perched raven shows on a phone too (56 px, on the head band, taking no tap); the head page's stays off it, since it sits on the gate.
- **Frosted glass** (todo #138). Where the browser supports `backdrop-filter`, Raven's large panels (a base on the map, the comms and the head page, the dossier and timeline, a settings group, a sheet) take a translucent fill and blur the picture behind them (8 px), so the castle shows through; without it they keep the opaque fills. Text keeps 4.5 : 1 over the brightest part of the picture at the lightning's peak (measured). Standard and Geek do not change.
- **The raven's head turn can be seen, and the castle shows behind the head page** (todo #138, round 2). The turned frame was the profile's twin at its resting dark, so at real size the turn did not read. While it shows, the raven is now lit by the moon: its own colours instead of the profile's dark, a cool rim of moonlight round it, a glint in its eye, and 3 s on the turned frame. On a desktop the perch on the map is 100 px wide (it was 70), on a phone 56 (it was 44). The head page's comms were one 72 percent panel over the whole picture, so the castle was mush behind it; its glass is now 18 percent over a 2 px blur, the rows that stand on it bare (the chapter rule, the times, a bubble, a notice, a tool card, the notes and the queue) carry a darker backing of their own, and text keeps 4.5 : 1 (measured over the brightest lit pixel). On a phone the head page also opens a strip of the picture, up to 120 px, between the header and the conversation. Standard and Geek do not change.
- **Lightning that looks like lightning, a perch the turn can be seen from, glass you can see through** (todo #138, round 3; the boss still saw no thunder or lightning: the flash was a 17 to 29 of 255 brightening). The strike now draws a forked bolt in the sky and a violet wash on the map and the head page, as a double pulse of 0.27 s on the same 61 s clock and 7 s first strike as the picture's light and the thunder (one more fading layer, no repaint; reduced motion draws neither bolt nor wash, calm and phones follow the rules above). What stands bare on the picture (the crumbs, the bar's buttons, the legend, the new-base button, the head page's rows) has a darker backing, so text keeps 4.5 : 1 under the bolt's white core (walked in a browser at the peak: 4.99 : 1 at the lowest). The head page's perched raven stands in the margin left of the log, on the painting's left cross and facing the log (it sat behind the message bubbles); where the window has no such margin (1380 px and under) it is not drawn. The map's glass is 60 percent (it was 72), so the castle reads through the bases. The header's height for the comms (`--hud`) now follows a theme switch: the observer watches the border box it reads, and Raven's thicker top rule left the comms 3 px short after Raven to Standard until a reload (a general fix, not Raven's).
