# pi-straw-boss 0.60.3

Not yet released; the release commit sets the version and date.

## A slow Herdr at start no longer leaves a session off the net

A main (the "sb 主塔台實體化" session) reloaded at load 30 to 50 and came back with no `sb_*` pane tokens and none of the joined-only tools (`boss_status`, `board_set`); other mains in the same workspace kept theirs, and intercom stayed connected. The start asks Herdr where the session runs (`pane current`, then the workspace list) before it joins; one of those calls timed out, and the session took the failure as an answer: it barred itself until the next reload.

- **Only a definitive answer bars.** A head workspace or a sub workspace bars for good, as before. A Herdr call that failed leaves the session unplaced, not barred: the boss gets one warning ("could not tell whether this is a sub workspace: …; it asks again"), and the check is asked again after 1 s, 2 s, 5 s, then every 15 s.
- **The boss does not wait for the timer.** `/boss` and a `subagent` or `subagent_resume` call ask again at once (or share the ask already in flight) before they are refused. (No tool exists before the join: `boss_promote` and the net tools are added by it.)
- **A failed join is retried too.** A join that failed at start (intercom not connected within 15 s, no owner elected, or the second look at the pane failing before any channel exists) is retried by the same timers; before, only a `connection` or `owner` event on the channel retried it, and a failure before the channel existed produced no event at all. A join that waiting cannot fix (pi-intercom not loaded, a tab id too long for a namespace, a broker without `extension-bus-v1`) is told once and not retried; `/boss` tries it again.
- **Once, and quietly.** When a retry succeeds the normal start runs once (join, restore watch, opener and restore handling): no second join, no second "joined" note. The boss gets one info line when the session joined after retries, and each warning (the check's, the join's, a pane without `terminal_id`) is told once per start. What the role record says is read at the first attempt only, so a retry does not forget the record a session wrote when `/boss` made it main while the retry waited. A `/new`, `/resume`, or shutdown ends the old start's retries (no timer outlives them, none keeps the process alive, and a failure of the old start's join or check that comes late tells and arms nothing).
- **Two old edges closed with it.** A check that answers after the session quit no longer joins it. A `/resume` into a tower's session file no longer keeps the bar of the member that was unplaced before, which let the tower's `subagent` calls through.

Wire: none. New dependency seam `joinRetryMs` (the waits; the last repeats).

## Zen: a card is never taken from you, and Enter presses the card's button

Boss, on 0.60.2: 「terminal prompt 的 confirm 無法用 enter，確認也無法用 enter，然後我正在操作的時候，被強制切呼吸打斷，我覺得不該卡片已經跳出來了，還強制切回呼吸」「新進來的卡片也是，應該放在背景等候排隊，永遠不要打斷使用者正在進行的操作」. Asked how far Enter should go, he chose 「全部卡片都用 Enter」. Decision Z-D20 (`docs/specs/2026-11-01-zen-view.md`, *Never interrupted, and Enter acts*) supersedes parts of Z-D19 and the old "No default Allow".

- **A new card never takes the screen by itself.** In 0.60.2 only the breathing circle held a card back: with a session for company, with the head's whole conversation open (a wheel or swipe up on the circle, **H**, or the link), a card took the screen with the bowl. Now it queues everywhere. The cue says how many wait (under the head strip's input, under the name on a session's screen, on the head's whole conversation); **Enter**, or a tap on the cue, takes the next one. The terminal layer stays as it is when a card arrives. The page's reminder for a page nobody looks at stays, one per card. A card that resolves while it waits (answered in the terminal, the unit moved on) is never drawn, plays no bowl or knock, and leaves the count.
- **A card on the screen is never replaced or pulled away.** When its question ends while you read it (answered in the terminal, the unit's next question, the unit gone or closed, a snapshot gap) the card stays with the question you were reading, every answer on it off and *Already answered elsewhere* (a failed unit keeps its error line and low bowl). Its one button, *Back to the breath*, takes the focus; **Enter**, **Esc**, and a tap close it, then the pause and the breath; the unit's next question waits for Enter. The knock rings once; a question listed again by a later snapshot brings the card back to life with no second sound. Your own accepted answer still ends the card with its 1.2 s outcome and the pause. The 1.3 s inert card and the 20 s draft hold are gone, since nothing is drawn over a card and nothing waits to take a screen.
- **Enter presses the primary control of every Zen card.** When you take a card the focus lands on it: a dialog's first choice (a forwarded dialog's too), **Open the terminal**, **Trust**, **Handle**, **Allow**, **Confirm**, the pair card's number field (Enter submits the number), a terminal step's **Run** and a pane's line. Enter never fires while a text field has the focus (the companion's box, the head's input, a card's box, a terminal's key sink), and a focused button keeps its own Enter. A held Enter is kept from the button it landed on.
- **The key pad's confirm takes Enter in Zen.** The terminal layer's **Send Enter**, **Send Escape**, and **Send key** confirms take the focus on the button that sends; Enter confirms. A repeating key, an Enter whose keydown began before the confirm showed, and Space confirm nothing. Standard and Geek keep the focus on Cancel; the pad is shared code and the difference is one flag, set only when the page's view is Zen (the terminal card's confirm in Zen's card does the same).
- **Demo.** `?zen=settled` (the card's question is answered elsewhere `&settle=<ms>` on, 6000 by default) and `&box=<ms>` on any state (a box's question arrives that long after the page starts and queues behind whatever is shown).

Wire: none.
