## Trains in the net (task 5)

- The sb server reads the trains of `<state>/trainboard/` into the GUI's snapshot (`yards`) with the 1-minute load and the core count (`load`; none on Windows). Both stay on this computer and are off with the merge train off.
- A monitor that prints `{"progress":{"message","load"}}` shows its message and keeps its load.
- `cli.mjs train notices [--project P] [--pending] [--json]` lists every car's states, who set each, the line sent, the attempts, and when it was delivered or escalated.
