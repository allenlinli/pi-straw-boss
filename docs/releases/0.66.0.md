## Raven round 2

- **Square corners.** No card, panel, bar, tile, pill, button, field, or sheet of the Raven Keep view has a rounded corner (boss, 2026-11-08). Three blanket rules in `gui/themes/raven/raven.css` set `border-radius: 0 !important` under `html[data-view="raven"]`, so Standard's rules win nowhere; pi-better-show blocks follow (`--pbs-radius: 0`). What is round by meaning stays round: the status plate and tower with their rings, halos and badges, the avatar, the dots of a link, an ally unit, a toggle, a drawer name and a bar button, and a timeline mark that is done, joined, or left. `tests/raven-square.test.ts` pins that list.
- **Two ornaments, no more.** A gold double rule (4 px: a line, a gap, a line, the head band's own) on top of the panels (the HUD, bases, the comms, the settings groups, the net-activity panel, sheets, dialogs, the tower card, a unit's question), and four corner ticks on the cards that ask for an answer (sheets, the dock's dialog, the tower card, a unit's question). Both take the one gold-grey line colour `--rk-rule`. The ticks are background layers of the box, so a box that scrolls keeps them in its corners, and no Raven rule positions a dialog or a sheet (a modal one sits in the top layer, where `relative` would put it against the document). A question nested in the dock's dialog or a link alert wears neither ornament, and the yard (no padding) only the rule.
- **Rain and thunder.** With Sound on, a quiet rain loop plays under the backdrop (the map and the head page) and a low thunder follows each lightning flash after 1 to 2 s, a different wait each time. Both are synthesized on the page's audio core (`gui/themes/raven/weather.ts`; no files, no licence question), so Volume and the Sound switch govern them. The flash is the stylesheet's own clock: a no-op `rk-clap` animation delayed to the strike's instant, so the thunder cannot drift from the picture. Nothing sounds with Sound off, on a hidden tab, before the first press, off the backdrop, or under calm (the rain fades out when the page goes calm and returns when calm ends: a page nobody touches stops working); no thunder under reduced motion either, where the lightning is off; the rain stops when the view or the page leaves the backdrop. The page's press, key, and animation hooks are on the document only while the weather can sound, a flash event that arrives late (a stalled page, or the first frames after a hidden tab came back) makes no thunder, and a rain stopped in its fade-in falls from the level it had reached. Measured through an offline render: rain RMS -39 dBFS, thunder peak -25 dBFS, against the caw's RMS -28 and peak -17 and the bell's RMS -18 and peak -3.

## The work board on the head page (todo #135; [spec](../specs/2026-11-09-board-view.md))

Until now only towers could read the work board. The head tower's page now has a **Work board** section, and `/boss board` prints the same rows in pi. How it works: [The work board](../how-it-works.md#the-work-board).

- **Ended lines stay in view.** A line whose session is no longer a live main shows as ended for as long as its file exists (a tower's `board_read` still drops it after 30 minutes), so the boss can see what was not cleaned up. A live main that wrote nothing shows too, as "not written".
- **Ask progress.** On a live main's row, one tap sends it a fixed question (reply in two or three lines in Traditional Chinese, update the board line) and reads "Asked" for a minute.
- **Clear.** For an ended line, or one whose idle main has not touched it for over 2 hours, after a confirm that names the main and the line. A writer whose session is still open (an idle main, or a session that is no longer a main) is told once that its line was cleared, and the page says whether the notice was delivered or only queued; a closed session is told nothing. The button is disabled while the request is on its way.
- **`/boss board`** prints a main that commands work as `commanding`, as the page does.
- **Local only.** Allies' boards are not shown there, and the board never goes to Straw Link, a remote turn or an operate session. A paired device sees it and can ask and clear, as it can send input.
